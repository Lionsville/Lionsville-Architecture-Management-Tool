## The organisation's tree starts folded

- **A home lists the scopes directly under it.** The tree of scopes on the
  organisation screen, and on every domain's home, starts folded: each domain
  is one row that says how many scopes are under it, and its chevron opens it.
  **Show all** above the tree opens every level at once and **Fold all** shuts
  them again. What you open stays open while the app is.
- **The chevron is easier to press.** It is now a target the height of its row
  rather than a 22-pixel caret.
