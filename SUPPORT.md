# Support

**Questions** go to the repository's
[Discussions](https://github.com/Lionsville/Lionsville-Architecture-Management-Tool/discussions):
how something works, whether something is possible, how you would do a thing.
The manual is in `docs/`, in four languages, and answers most of them.

**Bugs** go to the
[issues](https://github.com/Lionsville/Lionsville-Architecture-Management-Tool/issues),
on the bug report form. Something the tool should do and does not is a feature
request, on its form, or a discussion first if it is big.

**Security problems** go nowhere public; `SECURITY.md` says where.

There is no paid support through this repository, and no guaranteed response
time for anything above. Issues and discussions are read by the maintainers
when they can.
